## 1. Background

Maple Prairie Bank serves about 310,000 customers through 42 branches, a contact centre, online banking and a mobile app. In 2024 it received 2,440 complaints through six channels. Complaints are handled by frontline staff (Level 1) and, when unresolved, by the Complaints Office (Level 2). Customers who remain unhappy can go to the external complaints body, the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments (OBSI).

As a federally regulated bank, Maple Prairie Bank must follow the complaint handling requirements of the Financial Consumer Protection Framework in the Bank Act, overseen by the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada (FCAC). These include giving the customer a final response within **56 days** of receiving the complaint, or explaining the delay and telling them about OBSI, and keeping complete records of every complaint.

## 2. Problem statement

An internal audit in late 2024 found that the complaint process was inconsistent across channels, that some complaints were logged days after they were received, that escalated complaints often missed the 56 day deadline, and that root cause data was too incomplete to fix recurring problems.

## 3. Business objectives

| # | Objective | Measure | Baseline (2024) | Target (Q4 2025) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BO1 | Meet the 56 day final response requirement for every complaint | Closed within 56 days | 92.0% | 100% |
| BO2 | Acknowledge every complaint quickly | Acknowledged within 2 days | 62.8% | 98% |
| BO3 | Capture complaints when they happen | Logged within 1 day of receipt | 63.5% | 98% |
| BO4 | Fix problems at the source | Records with a root cause | 94.2% | 100% |
| BO5 | Reduce repeat fee complaints | Complaints from the top 3 fee root causes | 501 | 350 (about 30% lower) |

## 4. Scope

**In scope**
* Complaint capture in all six channels (branch, phone, email, web form, social media, letter)
* Acknowledgement, routing, escalation and deadline tracking
* Complaint records, root cause and outcome data
* Management and regulatory reporting
* Updated procedures and training

**Out of scope**
* Replacing the CRM platform (the complaints module will be configured within it)
* Changes to fee schedules or products (root cause findings will be passed to product owners)
* Fraud investigation procedures themselves (only how fraud complaints are routed and tracked)

## 6. Assumptions and constraints

* The CRM vendor can configure a complaints module without custom code
* Complaint data from 2024 is available for analysis
* Changes must be live before the bank's next FCAC reporting cycle
* Customer data stays in Canada
