# Gap Analysis
## Complaint Management Process Redesign

Compares the current state with the agreed future state and names what must change to close each gap.

| # | Capability | Current state | Future state | Gap | Closed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Capture in every channel | Phone and web in CRM; branch on paper; email, letter and social re keyed | Every channel creates a CRM record at the point of contact | Branch, email, letter and social capture | BR01 |
| G2 | Regulatory clock | Deadlines counted from logged date in a spreadsheet | Received date captured; system calculates day 56 | No received date field; manual deadlines | BR02, BR04 |
| G3 | Acknowledgement | Automatic only for phone and web | Automatic for all channels, English and French | Templates and triggers for 4 channels | BR03 |
| G4 | Deadline warnings | None | Alerts at day 30, 45 and 50; escalation to manager | Alert rules and dashboard | BR04 |
| G5 | Delay notice and OBSI information | Sent manually, sometimes late | Generated before day 56 if case still open | Template and trigger | BR05 |
| G6 | Hand offs to other teams | Email with no tracking | Linked tasks with owners, due dates and "waiting on external party" status | Task workflow | BR06 |
| G7 | Fraud and disputes routing | Same queue as all other complaints | Routed to specialist queue with higher priority | Routing rule | BR07 |
| G8 | Duplicates | Not detected | Possible duplicates flagged at capture | Matching rule | BR08 |
| G9 | Root cause | Optional | Mandatory on closure, from a controlled list | Field validation and list | BR09 |
| G10 | Insight to product owners | None | Monthly root cause dashboard and review | Dashboard and governance | BR10 |
| G11 | Complaint definition | Unclear; 44% of branch staff unsure | One definition, simple procedure, training for all frontline staff | Procedure and training | BR11 |
| G12 | Audit trail and reporting | Partial; spreadsheet outside the CRM | Full history in CRM; regulatory and management reports | Audit and report configuration | BR12 |

## Change impact by group

| Group | What changes | Impact | Support needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branch staff | Log complaints at the teller workstation instead of a paper form | Medium | 30 minute training, quick reference card |
| Contact centre | Minor: new mandatory fields, duplicate warnings | Low | Short briefing |
| Complaints Office | Work from CRM queue and dashboard, no spreadsheet | High | Hands on training, super user |
| Fraud and Disputes | Receive and update linked tasks in CRM | Medium | Training and agreed timelines |
| Digital team | Create CRM record from social posts | Medium | Training, templates |
| Product owners | Monthly root cause review | Low | Dashboard walkthrough |
