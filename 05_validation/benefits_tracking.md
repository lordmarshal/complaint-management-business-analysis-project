# Benefits Tracking
## Complaint Management Process Redesign

Go live: August 25, 2025. Benefits measured for Q4 2025 (October 1 to December 31) and compared with Q4 2024 from the same SQL queries, so seasonality does not distort the comparison.

## Results against business objectives

| Objective | Measure | Baseline | Target | Q4 2025 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BO1 | Closed within 56 days | 92.0% (2024) | 100% | **99.6%** | 🟠 Close |
| BO2 | Acknowledged within 2 days | 62.8% (2024) | 98% | **98.9%** | 🟢 Met |
| BO3 | Logged within 1 day of receipt | 63.5% (2024) | 98% | **97.4%** | 🟠 Close |
| BO4 | Closed records with a root cause | 94.2% (2024) | 100% | **100%** | 🟢 Met |
| BO5 | Complaints from the top 3 fee root causes | 123 (Q4 2024) | about 30% lower | **84 (31.7% lower)** | 🟢 Met |

## Notes on the results

* **BO1:** 2 of 512 closed complaints went past day 56. Both were fraud claims waiting on a card network, and both customers received delay notices with OBSI information before day 56, as required.
* **BO3:** the remaining late logging is concentrated in three busy branches on Saturdays. Action: a quick capture option on tablets in those branches (Q1 2026).
* **BO5:** the fee product owner fixed the exemption rule error and rewrote overdraft and e-Transfer fee disclosures after the first monthly root cause review.

## Benefit we did not expect

Total complaints recorded in Q4 2025 rose to **640** from 615 in Q4 2024. This is a sign of better capture, not worse service: branch complaints resolved on the spot are now recorded (RULE01), which is what the CCO wanted.
